# MQ-2 Market Vision Spatial Encoding

## Purpose

Convert normalized market features into deterministic spatial stimulation of
the frozen MQ-001 R1-R6 retinal territories.

This stage defines sensory representation only.

It does not contain BUY, HOLD, SELL, bullish, bearish, profitable, dangerous,
good, or bad semantics.

## Spatial Encoding v1

The first implementation encodes four spatial dimensions.

### Return

Property:

`horizontal_position`

Signed.

Positive and negative values move the stimulus in opposite horizontal
directions.

Sign must not alter total sensory energy.

### Volume Deviation

Property:

`vertical_position`

Signed.

Positive and negative values move the stimulus in opposite vertical
directions.

Sign must not alter total sensory energy.

### Spread

Property:

`spatial_diffusion`

Unsigned magnitude.

Greater magnitude increases spatial width while total sensory energy is
renormalized.

### Order-Book Imbalance

Property:

`local_horizontal_skew`

Signed.

Opposite signs skew the local pattern in opposite horizontal directions.

Sign must not alter total sensory energy.

## Temporal Features

The following are intentionally deferred until the spatial encoder is frozen:

## Energy Rule

Every active ticker territory receives the same base integrated sensory energy
before later temporal modulation.

Spatial transformations redistribute that energy across receptors but do not
change the total merely because a signed feature is positive or negative.

This prevents directional market values from acquiring accidental
reward/punishment semantics through brightness.

## Scientific Boundary

The mapping from market variables to visual properties is artificial.

The stimulated receptors, retinal geometry, and downstream connectome are
biological-data-derived.

Neural responses must not be inspected and then used to tune this mapping.
