## Interpretation

The cumulative attenuation curve measures how much of the observed effect is
captured by the empirically identified first-wave network.

Possible outcomes include:

- strongly cumulative mediation;
- diminishing returns from overlapping routes;
- early saturation;
- incomplete explanation after all tested branches.

No assumption of independence is permitted.

## Claims excluded

This experiment does not establish:

- that attenuation percentages are additive;
- exclusive pathway usage;
- complete biological mechanism;
- biological learning in a living organism;
- subjective experience;
- financial utility;
- improved trading performance.
