**Canonical status:** Provisional scientific subject program

## Why this exists

SHUFFLED MOSCA asks whether observed behavior depends on biological topology
relative to randomized controls.

MQ-002 asks a different question:

> What changes when the frozen MoscaQuant scientific interface is applied to a
> second real Drosophila biological topology under defensibly matched
> conditions?

These control families complement one another and must not be conflated.

The program may grow only as evidence permits:

1. `MQ-002.0 — IDENTITY CHECK`
   - acquire and validate official cross-mappings;
   - quantify overlap with MQ-001's frozen scientific interface;
   - identify unmatched anatomy explicitly.

2. `SQ-03A — THE OTHER FLY`
   - first result-bearing matched biological-topology comparison;
   - exact scope frozen only after MQ-002.0.

3. Later comparisons may separately test:
   - isomorphic-only connectivity;
   - explicitly dimorphic connectivity;
   - sensory-transfer compatibility;
   - causal-perturbation transfer;
   - plasticity-rule transfer;
   - matched readout divergence.

These later items are ideas, not authorized experiments.
