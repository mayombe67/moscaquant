# MQ-7.8 SHOCK × Plasticity Interaction Protocol

## Research question

Can acute SC-01 stimulation of neuron 68045 expose a persistent SC-03
plasticity effect under market replay condition B, where the same plasticity
has previously produced no measurable replay-level divergence?

## Motivation

During the frozen full-D6 multi-session experiment, condition B was usually
insensitive to the validated 56393 -> 68045 SC-03 plasticity overlay.

However, mq7-full-d6-048 combined:

- market condition B;
- persistent SC-03 multiplier near 0.95;
- matched SC-01 SHOCK;
- SHOCK target 68045;

and O1/O2 voltage and anonymous readout scores diverged.

This experiment isolates that observation.

## Design

Market replay:

B

Frozen plasticized edge:

56393 -> 68045

Plasticity multiplier:

0.95

Acute perturbation target:

68045

SC-01 intensity:

0.75 activity floor

SC-01 duration:

1 transition

Intervention generation:

32

## Conditions

B0:

- no SC-03 plasticity;
- no SHOCK.

BP:

- SC-03 multiplier 0.95;
- no SHOCK.

BS:

- no SC-03 plasticity;
- SHOCK target 68045.

BPS:

- SC-03 multiplier 0.95;
- identical SHOCK target 68045.

## Primary comparisons

1. B0 vs BP

Tests whether plasticity alone is measurable under condition B.

2. BS vs BPS

Tests whether the same plasticity becomes measurable when 68045 is acutely
stimulated.

## Interaction interpretation

Evidence consistent with perturbation-dependent expression requires:

- B0 and BP remain identical or substantially less divergent;
- BS and BPS diverge at one or more predefined neural/readout levels.

This experiment does not assume statistical synergy.

A formal synergy claim is not permitted from this single targeted experiment.

## Measurements

- voltage trace hash;
- spike trace hash;
- anonymous readout scores;
- final anonymous decision;
- score deltas;
- structural connectome digest.

## Required invariants

- BS and BPS receive exactly the same SHOCK intervention.
- SHOCK target is exactly neuron 68045.
- SHOCK timing and intensity are identical.
- The SC-03 overlay is the only difference between BS and BPS.
- The structural connectome must remain unchanged.
- No post-hoc target substitution is permitted.

## Claims excluded

This experiment does not establish:

- financial utility;
- improved trading;
- biological learning in a living organism;
- emotional response;
- pain or reward;
- general intelligence;
- formal nonlinear synergy.

Null results are valid.
