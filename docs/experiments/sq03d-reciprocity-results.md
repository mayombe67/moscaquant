## Interpretation

The full deterministic census is dominated by extremely small context
residuals, but it contains a pronounced upper tail.

The strongest paired observations show that the same matched male/female
edge-weight difference can have materially different modeled consequences when
embedded in the two frozen matched-network backgrounds.

The global normalized-asymmetry statistic is not interpreted by itself because
very small effects can produce extreme ratios. Raw context residual and total
effect magnitude remain the primary interpretive quantities.

SQ-03D is a model-level paired-background result. It does not establish a
biological sex mechanism, organism-level behavior, population-level
statistical significance, intelligence differences, or financial utility.

The strongest context-residual pairs require independent scalar verification
before they are treated as verified SQ-03D hotspots.
