# MQ-7 Causal-Pathway Closure Addendum

## Status

CLOSED AFTER MQ-7.19

## Reason for reopening

The MQ-7 causal-pathway subphase was temporarily reopened after external
technical feedback identified a concrete unresolved question:

Could partially redundant downstream routes compensate for one another in a
way that makes single-route ablations misleading?

That question was converted into two bounded experiments:

- MQ-7.18 Pairwise Route Interaction / Compensation
- MQ-7.19 Conditional Pairwise Contribution

## Result

MQ-7.18 found structured supra-independent interaction among multiple
first-wave branch pairs, with zero interaction excess in the validated null-edge
pair controls.

MQ-7.19 then tested the three strongest frozen pairs and found positive
conditional gain in both directions for every pair, with zero conditional gain
in the null controls.

## Supported conclusion

Under the frozen replay, the perturbation-dependent SC-03 DN-C1 effect is:

- distributed across a dynamically identified first-wave subnetwork;
- non-additive;
- structured at the pairwise level;
- conditionally dependent among the strongest tested branch pairs.

The final phrase is intentionally limited to the model.

Biological compensation is not established.

## Stop rule

No blind triple or higher-order combinatorial search follows from MQ-7.19.

The causal-pathway subphase is closed unless a future independent hypothesis
requires reopening it.
