## Interpretation

The perturbation-dependent SC-03 effect is not carried by a single exclusive
downstream route.

Instead, the effect is distributed across a small empirically identified
first-wave subnetwork.

The ten first-wave branches identified from dynamic tracing collectively
accounted for approximately 96.5% of the measured DN-C1 effect.

Adding the independently validated 68045 -> 1273 route reduced the residual
effect to approximately 2.8e-7 of the intact magnitude.

This supports distributed but structured causal mediation.

## Important non-additivity

Individual branch attenuation percentages must not be summed.

The cumulative experiment directly measured the combined effect and revealed
substantial overlap and diminishing returns among branches.
