# MQ-7.9 SHOCK Target-Specificity Protocol

## Research question

Is the perturbation-dependent expression observed in MQ-7.8 specific to acute
stimulation of neuron 68045, or can stimulation of other frozen SC-01 targets
also expose the same persistent SC-03 plasticity state?

## Background

MQ-7.8 established that:

- market condition B alone does not measurably express the validated
  56393 -> 68045 plasticity overlay;
- SHOCK of neuron 68045 produces a measurable replay effect;
- with the same SHOCK applied, adding the 0.95 SC-03 overlay produces additional
  voltage/readout divergence.

This experiment tests target dependence.

## Conditions per target

For every target T:

BS(T):
- SHOCK target T
- no SC-03 plasticity

BPS(T):
- identical SHOCK target T
- SC-03 multiplier 0.95

The only difference between BS(T) and BPS(T) is the persistent SC-03 overlay.

A common B0 baseline and BP plasticity-only replay are also recorded.

## Primary measurement

For each target:

BS(T) vs BPS(T)

Record:

- voltage-trace equality;
- spike-trace equality;
- readout-score equality;
- final decision equality;
- DN-C0 score delta;
- DN-C1 score delta;
- DN-C2 score delta.

## Interpretation

A target exposes the latent SC-03 state when BS(T) and BPS(T) differ at a
predefined neural/readout level.

Possible outcomes include:

- 68045 uniquely exposes the plasticized state;
- a subset of targets exposes the state;
- many targets expose the state;
- no target-dependent pattern is observed.

The result must be reported as target-dependent expression unless stronger
evidence is obtained.

This experiment does not establish formal nonlinear synergy.

## Required invariants

- market replay is identical for every condition;
- intervention generation is identical;
- SHOCK intensity is identical;
- SC-03 multiplier is exactly 0.95;
- the same target receives SHOCK in matched BS/BPS pairs;
- no post-hoc target selection is permitted;
- the structural connectome remains unchanged.

## Claims excluded

This experiment does not establish:

- financial utility;
- improved decision quality;
- biological learning in a living organism;
- pain, memory recall, or subjective experience;
- formal synergy;
- anatomical uniqueness from a single experiment.
