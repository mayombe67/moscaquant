# SQ-06 — SILENT CARTOGRAPHER — Preregistration Review Candidate

## Why this study exists

SQ-05 is sealed. Its post-seal descriptive orientation audit does not alter that
result.

The audit found a striking but explicitly post-result pattern inside the
historical 13-edge lesion:

- in LR, only six accepted responders changed: `51, 55, 92, 129, 656, 1273`;
- in RL, only three accepted responders changed: `317, 126002, 137122`;
- the two responder sets are disjoint and their union is the accepted nine;
- the corresponding historical lesion edges partition 10 edges versus 3 edges;
- all measured lesion effect occurred after Cue B onset;
- all measured lesion effect was confined to DN-C1;
- the accepted nine accounted for 100% of the lesion-effect energy in both layouts.

This pattern was discovered after SQ-05 results existed. It is therefore not
independent confirmatory evidence. SQ-06 freezes it now for a new prospective
validation.

## Scientific question

Does the historical 13-edge lesion decompose into two orientation-selective
edge groups under the frozen SQ-05 visual sequence?

## Frozen edge groups

`LR_OBSERVED` contains the 10 historical lesion edges associated with the six
accepted responders that changed in SQ-05 LR.

`RL_OBSERVED` contains the 3 historical lesion edges associated with the three
accepted responders that changed in SQ-05 RL.

The groups are disjoint and their union is exactly the historical 13-edge
lesion. Each targeted edge carries its already-frozen matched sham partner.

No edge may move between groups after SQ-06 execution.

## Design

Seven arms are crossed with both frozen SQ-05 layouts:

1. `INTACT`
2. `FULL13_TARGETED`
3. `FULL13_SHAM`
4. `LR_GROUP_TARGETED`
5. `LR_GROUP_SHAM`
6. `RL_GROUP_TARGETED`
7. `RL_GROUP_SHAM`

Every arm/layout is replayed twice from fresh runtime state.

Total planned episodes: **28**.

The visual stimulus, 1,191-DN anatomy-only primary population, positive-voltage
fingerprint, and exact-reproduction tolerances are inherited prospectively from
the already-sealed SQ-05 protocol.

## Primary tests

The experiment does not add a post-result minimum-effect floor.

Instead it tests the stronger decomposition pattern directly:

- FULL13 remains a positive control relative to FULL13_SHAM in each layout.
- Both subset shams must exactly reproduce INTACT in both layouts.
- In LR, `LR_GROUP_TARGETED` must exactly reproduce `FULL13_TARGETED`.
- In LR, `RL_GROUP_TARGETED` must exactly reproduce `INTACT`.
- In RL, `RL_GROUP_TARGETED` must exactly reproduce `FULL13_TARGETED`.
- In RL, `LR_GROUP_TARGETED` must exactly reproduce `INTACT`.

Exact reproduction requires both:

- symmetric normalized L2 `<= 1e-9`;
- maximum absolute difference `<= 1e-12`.

Each primary condition is reported separately. The protocol may additionally
record whether all primary conditions were satisfied, but there is no
scientific "winner."

## Why this comes before a broad topology dose-response

SQ-05's lesion asymmetry turned out not to be diffuse. It was concentrated in
tiny, non-overlapping accepted-responder sets, with one DN dominating each
layout's absolute lesion effect.

Before launching a larger 0.5x/1x/2x/4x topology study, the cleaner question is
whether that apparent orientation partition prospectively reproduces when the
13-edge lesion is split along the already-observed boundary.

If it does, a later topology-dose experiment can treat layout as a first-class
factor with a much clearer mechanistic target.

If it does not, the SQ-05 partition remains a descriptive post-result pattern
and must not be promoted into a circuit rule.

## Boundaries

The groups are explicitly outcome-derived from SQ-05.

SQ-06 therefore tests prospective reproducibility of a prior observation; it
does not transform that observation into independent discovery.

No result may be described as fruit recognition, hunger, danger perception,
fear, behavior, a biological orientation circuit, or financial value.

No SQ-05 result is reopened or rewritten.
