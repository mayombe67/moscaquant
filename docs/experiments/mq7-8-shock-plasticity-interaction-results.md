## Interaction interpretation

Persistent SC-03 plasticity was silent under condition B without acute
perturbation.

When the postsynaptic node of the plasticized edge, neuron 68045, received
matched SC-01 stimulation, the same persistent plasticity became measurable at
the voltage and anonymous readout-score levels.

This supports perturbation-dependent expression of the persistent plasticized
state.

This experiment does not establish formal nonlinear synergy.

## Supported conclusions

- SC-03 plasticity can remain latent under a given replay condition.
- Acute stimulation of the affected pathway can expose that latent state.
- SC-01 targeting neuron 68045 can alter the final anonymous readout decision.
- The persistent plasticity modifies the SHOCK-driven neural trajectory.
- No structural connectome mutation is required.

- financial utility;
- improved decision quality;
- biological learning in a living organism;
- pain, trauma, memory recall, or subjective experience;
- formal nonlinear synergy;
- superiority of the adaptive condition.
