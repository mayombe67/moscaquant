# MQ-8.5B — HR Evidence Records Protocol

## Purpose

MoscaQuant HR records are narrative and organizational artifacts tied to
documented evidence.

They may cite scientific, achievement, containment, or canonical milestone
records.

They do **not** create scientific evidence.

## Allowed actions

- COMMENDATION
- PROMOTION
- DEMOTION
- PIP
- DISCIPLINE
- REVIEW

## Evidence rule

COMMENDATION, PROMOTION, DEMOTION, PIP, and DISCIPLINE require at least one
evidence reference.

REVIEW may exist without a formal evidence reference when it is explicitly
editorial or procedural.

## Evidence reference kinds

- ACHIEVEMENT
- EXPERIMENT
- CONTAINMENT_EVENT
- CANON_MILESTONE

## Scientific boundary

An HR record may summarize or cite a scientific result, but the scientific
meaning remains governed by the underlying experiment record, achievement
science text, claim boundary, and evidence level.

HR cannot:

- promote a scientific claim to a higher evidence level
- override a null result
- rewrite experiment history
- change WARDEN containment
- alter MORTY
- alter GLaDOS / ORACLE
- create or modify achievement occurrences
- manufacture scientific provenance

## Presentation

Public HR records may appear in Panopticon personnel dossiers, the live blog,
achievement dossiers, or BroadcastEvent entries.

The absurdity may be loud.
The evidence linkage must remain exact.

## First canonical record set — v1

The initial canonical HR registry contains four records:

- HR-001 — GLaDOS / COMMENDATION / Productive Interference
- HR-002 — Placeholder McDoctorate / REVIEW / Replication Requested
- HR-003 — WARDEN / COMMENDATION / Containment Without Negotiation
- HR-004 — MQ-001 / REVIEW / Performance Review: Subject Remains the Subject

No promotion, demotion, PIP, or discipline action is issued in this first set.

This is intentional.

The schema supports those actions, but MoscaQuant does not manufacture personnel
history merely because a field exists.
