# ADR-0001: Male CNS Connectome

**Status:** Accepted

MQ-001 uses the complete adult male Drosophila CNS connectome as its structural neural topology.

The connectome provides biological structure, not a complete dynamic brain simulation. Neural dynamics, sensory mappings, decision mappings, plasticity, and embodiment are modeled components and must be identified as such.

Whole-connectome connectivity must remain sparse. Dense adjacency representations are prohibited.
